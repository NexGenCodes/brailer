# Auth screen — CEDAR & LEAD

A store-branded sign-in screen for an independent pencil company ("Cedar &
Lead — graphite · wax · tools").

- Brand block: wordmark "CEDAR & LEAD" and the tagline as an eyebrow above a form.
- One headline: "Welcome back." — one supporting line about the shop.
- Form card: email, password, a "Forgot password?" link and a red "Sign in" CTA.
- A trust line at the bottom: "Secure checkout · 30-day returns".
- Desktop 1440, mobile 390. Two frames, same content, mobile-stacked.
